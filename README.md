## My Favorite Series: *Suits*

**Genre:** Legal drama
**Network:** USA Network
**Run:** 2011–2019 · 9 seasons · 134 episodes
**Created by:** Aaron Korsh

### What It's About
*Suits* follows **Mike Ross**, a brilliant college dropout with a photographic memory, who lands a job at a top New York law firm by pretending to have a Harvard law degree. He works under **Harvey Specter**, the firm's best closer, and the two have to keep Mike's secret while winning cases.

### Main Characters
| Character | Actor | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Harvey Specter | Gabriel Macht | Senior partner and top closer |
| Mike Ross | Patrick J. Adams | Associate with a photographic memory |
| Louis Litt | Rick Hoffman | Partner and finance expert |
| Donna Paulsen | Sarah Rafferty | Harvey's legal secretary |
| Jessica Pearson | Gina Torres | Managing partner |
| Rachel Zane | Meghan Markle | Paralegal |

### Why I Like It
1. The **witty dialogue** and fast pace
2. The friendship between Harvey and Mike
3. Clever cases and negotiations
4. Stylish suits and sets

### Things I Enjoy Most
- Courtroom and boardroom showdowns
- Plot twists
- The *"will Mike get caught?"* tension
  - Every season raises the stakes

> A story about talent, loyalty, and keeping a secret that could end everything.
